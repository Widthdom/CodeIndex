- **Batch is now a complete structured read-only query transport (#4582)** — the schema-owned side-effect-free allowlist now includes `goto` and `audit`; `--json-summary` embeds successful JSON as `result` and NDJSON as `results`, retains raw text for non-JSON or failed output, and enforces an auditable aggregate child-output budget.

## 日本語
