## English

- **Branch-switch refreshes now converge from the persisted whole-workspace baseline (#5054)** — `--changed-between` and current-HEAD commit refreshes reconcile paths omitted when the stored baseline is older than or divergent from the caller's old ref, while `workspace_verified_head_sha` keeps full-workspace proof separate from the latest scoped-write HEAD. Scoped mutations carry their affected paths into the next verified Git refresh even when a later commit range has no net diff; partial or bounded-overflow coverage fails closed until a normal full scan succeeds. Successful CLI and MCP full scans publish that verified state without retaining the pre-scan `head_changed` rebuild warning; sparse-checkout paths that cannot be materialized conservatively preserve the prior verification. Status, map, and symbol analysis read persisted provenance with their response bodies from one SQLite snapshot, and MCP default, compact, and count projections expose the same provenance fields, so status, map, query-envelope, and MCP projections agree without requiring a rebuild while preserving the scoped-refresh and indexed-HEAD compatibility established by #2809 and #4854.
