## English

- **Branch-switch refreshes now converge from the persisted whole-workspace baseline (#5054)** — `--changed-between` and current-HEAD commit refreshes reconcile paths omitted when the stored baseline is older than or divergent from the caller's old ref, while `workspace_verified_head_sha` keeps full-workspace proof separate from the latest scoped-write HEAD. Successful full scans publish that verified state without retaining the pre-scan `head_changed` rebuild warning. Status, map, query-envelope, and MCP projections therefore agree without requiring a rebuild, preserving the scoped-refresh and indexed-HEAD compatibility established by #2809 and #4854.

## 日本語

- **branch switch 後の refresh が永続化済み workspace 全体基準から収束するよう修正しました (#5054)** — `--changed-between` と現在 HEAD の commit refresh は、保存基準が caller の old ref より古い場合や分岐している場合に不足 path を補完し、`workspace_verified_head_sha` で workspace 全体の検証値を最新 scoped-write HEAD から分離します。full scan 成功時は scan 前の `head_changed` rebuild 警告を残さず検証済み状態を公開します。これにより rebuild を要求せず status、map、query envelope、MCP projection が一致し、#2809 と #4854 で導入した scoped refresh / indexed HEAD 互換性も維持します。
