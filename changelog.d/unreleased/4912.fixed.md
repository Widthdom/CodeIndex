## English

- **Safety-audit guards now follow structural evidence instead of fixed line proximity (#4912)** — whole-file-read recipes accept same-path size/control guards and resolved bounded-writer helpers, while filesystem-traversal recipes resolve the `EnumerationOptions` value actually passed by the call. Accepted and rejected candidates now include decision, reason, relationship, subject, container, and evidence-path metadata, so unrelated variables, inverted checks, missing options, and unawaited helpers remain actionable findings.
