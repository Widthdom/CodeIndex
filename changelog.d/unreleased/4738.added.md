## English

- **Scientific and native-extension languages now expose reference graphs (#4738)** — Nim, MATLAB, Julia, D, Cython, and Ada emit bounded language-aware call, import/module, and type/base references. Ambiguous `.m` files preserve content-based classification and expose a conservative MATLAB/Objective-C symbol and reference union when evidence remains unresolved.
