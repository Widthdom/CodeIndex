## English

- **Dependency summaries distinguish returned counts from complete totals (#5346)** — `deps --summary-only --json` now reports count units, additional-result evidence, candidate coverage, query exhaustion, and total-count availability/authority. Bounded lookahead preserves existing candidate budgets; cycle summaries reuse their analysis totals, and incomplete graphs cannot make totals authoritative.

## 日本語

- **依存関係 summary が返却件数と完全な総件数を区別します (#5346)** — `deps --summary-only --json` は件数の単位、追加結果の証拠、候補範囲、検索完了、総件数の確定可否と authority を報告します。先読みは既存の候補上限を維持し、循環 summary は解析済みの総件数を再利用します。不完全なグラフでは総件数を authoritative としません。
