- **Symbol-kind-filtered indexes now persist and publish their coverage limits (#5224)** — CLI, workspace, and MCP readiness now share the normalized include/exclude policy and per-file dropped-symbol facts. Filtered generations and legacy databases without policy provenance no longer report negative symbol or graph results as authoritative; an unfiltered rebuild restores complete readiness.
