## English

- **Explicit projected JSON arrays preserve their schema (#5233)** — `search`, `symbols`, and `files` now keep `--json=array --fields ...` as a bare projected array, including empty and byte-bounded results, while preserving versioned errors and rejecting output combinations whose stream, metadata, diagnostics, or object shape is incompatible. This closes the residual array-selection regression from #5190.
