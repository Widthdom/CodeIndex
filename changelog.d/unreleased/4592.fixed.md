- **Scan, status, and watch now share membership and invalidation rules (#4592)** — `.cdidx` sidecars are consistently excluded from indexed source membership, while ignore files and workspace pattern/plugin inputs bypass watch filtering long enough to trigger a debounced full scan. Extractor reconciliation refreshes the process registry generation, unloading prior workspace plugins and reloading current pattern/plugin inputs while preserving explicitly registered host extractors. Ignore/unignore plus pattern extension edits, deletion, and plugin removal coverage keep reconciliation behavior aligned across all three paths.
