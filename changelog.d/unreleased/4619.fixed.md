## English

- **Scoped `deps` queries now filter before graph materialization (#4619)** — Exact-symbol, symbol-family, and noise filters are applied in SQLite before candidate ranking and limits for normal, cycle, and cross-workspace dependency reads. Long reads also honor command cancellation, and a bounded repository-scale regression guards latency and allocation.
