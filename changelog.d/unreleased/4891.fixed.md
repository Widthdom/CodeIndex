- **`status --explain` now covers every serialized top-level status field and major nested sections (#4891)** — accepted keys now come from the same source-generated serializer metadata as `status --json`; major readiness, trust, extractor, hook, maintenance, and reference-cap sections return structured meaning, source, dependencies, interpretation, and repair guidance, while dotted member paths and unknown-key candidates remain bounded and runtime-value-free.
